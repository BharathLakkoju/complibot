# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 pack (Annex A)

> **MVP maturity: `Preview · Limited control coverage`** (PRD §5). Not evaluated or claimed in depth until gold set v1. The metrics page shows "not yet measured".

**Copyright.** ISO/IEC 27001 and 27002 are © ISO/IEC. This pack uses **control numbers and short titles only**. Requirements are our own paraphrases of the control intent. Do not paste standard text or 27002 guidance.

**Structure.** The 2022 Annex A has 93 controls in four themes: Organizational (5.1–5.37), People (6.1–6.8), Physical (7.1–7.14), and Technological (8.1–8.34). This pack covers 20 high-value controls, the ones contracts, DPAs, and ISMS policies actually exercise. IDs are written `A.<theme>.<n>` to match common usage.

**Doc reality.** ISO 27001 certifies an ISMS, not a contract. Contract findings therefore answer "does this agreement give us what A.5.20 expects of a supplier agreement?", and policy findings answer "does this policy implement the control?". The pack also includes `ISO-CERT`, a vendor certification claim check (scope plus Statement of Applicability).

**Transition note.** ISO 27001:2013 certificates expired on 31 Oct 2025. A vendor citing a 2013 certificate (or Annex A numbering such as `A.9.x`, `A.12.x`) is a red flag.

**Verification:** every Annex A reference falls within the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ranges A.5.1–5.37, A.6.1–6.8, A.7.1–7.14 and A.8.1–8.34 (checked 2026-10-05). Cross-framework mappings are **indicative**: our judgment, not checked against an official crosswalk. Where they target SOC 2, the IDs were checked against AICPA TSP section 100 (2022 points of focus).
